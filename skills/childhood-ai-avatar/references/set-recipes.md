# Set Recipes

Use these plans as defaults. Adapt culturally specific props or clothing only when the user requests them or the identity source clearly supports them.

## Nine-expression set

Keep one visual style, background, crop, hairstyle family, and clothing family across all nine images.

| No. | Variant | Prompt fragment |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Natural smile | relaxed closed-mouth smile, direct gaze |
| 2 | Joyful smile | bright open smile, natural teeth, cheerful eyes |
| 3 | Playful pout | gentle playful pout, relaxed cheeks |
| 4 | Wink | one natural wink, other eye looking at camera |
| 5 | Side glance | subtle smile, eyes glancing slightly sideways |
| 6 | Curious surprise | softly raised brows, small rounded mouth, friendly surprise |
| 7 | Bashful laugh | warm laugh, gaze slightly lowered, natural cheeks |
| 8 | Cool pose | calm confident expression, simple child-safe sunglasses or no prop if accessories drift |
| 9 | Sleepy-cute | gentle sleepy eyes, tiny smile, no yawning distortion |

Avoid extreme expressions that materially reshape the face. Remove a prop when it hides identity-critical features.

## Four-expression set

Use variants 1, 2, 3, and 5 by default. If the example clearly features a signature effect such as a wink or sunglasses, replace variant 5 with that effect.

## Set consistency checklist

- Same apparent age and subject in every frame
- Same crop and head scale within a 5% visual tolerance
- Same background tone and lighting direction
- Same hairstyle family and base clothing
- No repeated image masquerading as a different variant
- No prop covering both eyes or the central facial structure

## Contact-sheet layouts

### 3x3

Place variants in numerical order, left to right and top to bottom. Use square cells and gutters around 2–3% of a cell width.

### 2x2

Place natural smile, joyful smile, playful pout, and side glance in reading order. Keep all heads aligned by eye line rather than by the top of the hair.
